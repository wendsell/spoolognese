# Spoolognese

**The sauce for your spools.** A self-hosted companion for [SimplyPrint](https://simplyprint.io/) users.

> **Work in progress.** Spoolognese is the next version of
> [simplyusage](https://github.com/wendsell/simplyusage), which works today.

## What it will do

- **Usage tracking.** Bambu Lab prints sent straight from Bambu Studio or Orca
  get their real per-color filament usage deducted from the right SimplyPrint
  spool. (Available now as simplyusage.)
- **Printable swatches.** Pick spools from your SimplyPrint inventory and
  download swatch models with brand, material, color, print temperature and
  spool ID on them. Works with any printer.
- **One small container,** on the same machine as your SimplyPrint bridge,
  with both features switchable.

Pairs well with [Spoolghetti](https://wendsell.github.io/spoolghetti/), for
finding the cheapest filament per kg on Amazon.
